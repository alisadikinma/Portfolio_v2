## Usage Guidelines

### Matching Emotions to Article Sections

| Article Section | Primary Emotion | Secondary | Intensity Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | Surprise, Fear, or Anger | Curiosity (Desire) | 0.80+ (high) |
| FORESHADOW | Anticipation | Desire | 0.70-0.85 (medium-high) |
| BODY (problem) | Sadness, Fear | Anger | 0.65-0.80 (medium) |
| BODY (solution) | Trust, Anticipation | Joy | 0.70-0.85 (medium-high) |
| PEAK | Joy, Surprise | Trust | 0.85+ (high) |
| CTA | Desire, Anticipation | Joy | 0.80+ (high) |

### Emotion Mixing Rules

1. **Never use more than 2 high-intensity emotions in one section** — it feels manipulative
2. **Alternate between positive and negative emotions** across sections for rhythm
3. **Trust words anchor the BODY** — they ground the emotional content in credibility
4. **Desire words drive the CTA** — "free", "exclusive", "secret" are conversion triggers
5. **Surprise words power the PEAK** — the climax needs genuine "aha" impact
