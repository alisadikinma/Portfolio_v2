ALI SADIKIN MA — PRESS KIT

== Bahasa Indonesia ==

Bio singkat
Ali Sadikin Ma adalah pakar AI Indonesia dari Batam, founder INDUSIA.ai. Ia memimpin pembangunan 56+ produk digital (AI, IoT, aplikasi mobile) di Satnusa dan juara 1 Global AI Demo Day 2026 di Bengaluru.

Bio lengkap
Ali Sadikin Ma adalah pakar AI Indonesia dari Batam dan founder INDUSIA.ai, perusahaan AI Indonesia untuk pabrik, logistik, dan ritel.

Kariernya dimulai sebagai engineer di perusahaan multinasional Singapura. Ia lalu mendirikan Marlin Booking, sistem tiket pelabuhan bersama Kementerian Perhubungan, yang membawanya ke UN-UNCTAD × Alibaba eFounders pada 2019. Di PT Sat Nusapersada, Batam, ia memimpin tim 31 orang yang mengirim 56+ produk digital enterprise (AI, IoT, aplikasi mobile).

Pada Januari 2026 ia juara 1 Global AI Demo Day di Bengaluru, India, dari 26 startup AI. Ia berbicara dalam bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris.

== English ==

Short bio
Ali Sadikin Ma is an AI expert from Batam, Indonesia, founder of INDUSIA.ai. He led the building of 56+ digital products (AI, IoT, mobile apps) at Satnusa and won 1st place at Global AI Demo Day 2026 in Bengaluru.

Full bio
Ali Sadikin Ma is an Indonesian AI expert from Batam and founder of INDUSIA.ai, an Indonesian AI company for factories, logistics and retail.

His career began as an engineer at a Singapore multinational. He then co-founded Marlin Booking, a port ticketing system built with the Ministry of Transportation, which led to UN-UNCTAD × Alibaba eFounders in 2019. At PT Sat Nusapersada in Batam he led a team of 31 that shipped 56+ enterprise digital products (AI, IoT, mobile apps).

In January 2026 he won 1st place at Global AI Demo Day in Bengaluru, India, among 26 AI startups. He speaks Indonesian and English.
